# Glass endorsement

Source ID: AUTO-END-GLASS-01

Endorsement: GLASS-01
FICTIONAL TRAINING WORDING. When listed on the selected policy version, this endorsement modifies
the base glazing wording to require separate review of the glass repair estimate. It does not
authorize payment, establish a deductible, or determine coverage for a particular loss.
The human handler must consider this endorsement with the applicable base wording.
